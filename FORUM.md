> I'm pretty sure they're the same. The M471A should be interchangeable with the M471
> I don't know if there's a M471B ... but I know the M471C is a full module. 
>
> The M471 is a stereo input, with stereo output (main) and then 2 AUX's
> The M471A is a stereo input, with stereo output (main) and then 3 AUX's
>
> Maybe yours has the traces, but no switch inside.
